ExcessMomᵢ = P[t-1] / P[t-6] − 1
VolAdjᵢ = ExcessMomᵢ × (Median(σ63) / σ63ᵢ)^0.25
Winsorᵢ = z-clip(VolAdjᵢ, −3, +3)
Rawᵢ = √FloatCapᵢ × ((1 + Winsorᵢ) / Median(1 + Winsor))³
Weightᵢ = cap-and-redistribute(Rawᵢ, 20%)
